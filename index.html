Sentient-EduCore: An In-Situ Affective Agentic Interface and Telemetry Architecture for Mitigating Educator Technostress

An interdisciplinary computing paradigm integrating Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Affective Computing, and Organizational Psychology to resolve teacher burnout and digital workload escalation.

View Source on GitHub

Executive Abstract

Occupational burnout and psychological distress among educators have escalated into a global sustainability crisis, threatening instructional quality and institutional retention across all educational tiers. While traditional interventions conceptualize burnout primarily through individual psychopathology or localized coping deficits, empirical investigations demonstrate that chronic educator strain is structurally driven by administrative creep, continuous parent-teacher connectivity, and the emergence of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) technostress. Drawing upon an exhaustive synthesis of organizational psychology paradigms (Maslach Burnout Inventory, Job Demands-Resources, and Conservation of Resources) alongside longitudinal shifts across five digital epochs, this project bridges empirical psychometrics with applied Human-Computer Interaction (HCI).

01. Task-Agent

Automated Scaffolding

Employs local Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to automate standardized 4-stage instructional lesson sequences and rubric derivation, saving 4–7 hours weekly.

02. Boundary-Guard

Right-to-Disconnect

An autonomous NLP-driven communication firewall buffering non-emergency parental transmissions outside contracted operational hours (18:00–07:00).

03. Affective Telemetry

In-Situ Micro-CBT

Non-invasive tracking of nocturnal interaction length and keystroke friction, triggering context-aware 60-second vagal reset prompts and automated draft checkpoints.

04. Policy Dashboard

Differential Privacy

Mathematically guarantees k-anonymity (k ≥ 10) to aggregate faculty-level workload indices and structural overtime risk alerts for educational administration.
